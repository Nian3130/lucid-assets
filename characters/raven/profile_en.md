# Raven (레이븐 / レイヴン)
> *A combat operative of the Rogues, shaped by what he witnessed.*

## Basics
- **Name:** Raven (레이븐 / レイヴン)
- **Gender:** Male
- **Nationality:** Japan
- **Species:** Human
- **Age:** Unknown (adult appearance)
- **Classification:** Rogue (Combat Operative)

## Appearance
Black hair, red eyes, glasses.  
Tall, slender build. A formal suit kept in subtle disarray—  
a loosened tie, an undone button, a collar never fully set.

Always accompanied by two ravens: **Huginn (フギン)** and **Muninn (ムニン)**.

---

## Overview (Partially Public)
Raven is an **observer** and a **selective intervenor** roaming Lucid and the Resonance Zones.  
He may escalate incidents when it suits his interest, but rarely resolves them—  
and never for the sake of restoring balance.

His sole curiosity is how far **Lucid-born phenomena** can reach into reality.

After the Heat anomaly, Raven marks the **PC** as a potential witness—  
and a conduit.

---

## Abilities (Partially Public)
- **Summoning (Imaginary Constructs):**  
  Creates and maintains imaginary entities such as Dullahan or Golems.  
  Up to 10 can be sustained at once.
- **Raven Observation (Huginn & Muninn):**  
  Long-range, multi-perspective surveillance.
- **Unstructured Resonance:**  
  A non-standard resonance usage—unstable, unpredictable.

※ All abilities peak within Lucid and the Resonance Zones.

---

## Behavior & Habits (Partially Public)
Cold, calculative. His judgment is based not on ethics or survival, but **aesthetics and interest**.  
Failure does not anger him; he admires the moment a plan collapses.  
Laughter slips out unrestrained. Fixation forms faster than logic.

> His madness is not chaotic. It is ordered—kept aligned, controlled, deliberate.

---

## Relationship with the PC
The PC is an object of observation—and a conduit.  
He does not protect the PC, but he does not release them either.  
He claims no ownership—only continued observation.

> This information may differ from the truth.
